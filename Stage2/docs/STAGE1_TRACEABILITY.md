### F10 / UC10 / SD05 (inspection portion)

These original result-returning signatures remain available. Current-session generation adds ChecklistEvaluation to carry provenance/status and generation identity. ChecklistPlanner supplies required evidence-based actions and the actual UC10 deterministic fallback. EvaluationSession stores immutable checklist/completion snapshots; completeChecklistItem never invokes AI.

InspectionChecklist.sections is refined from Map<String,List<String>> to Map<String,List<InspectionChecklistItem>> for stable IDs, action, priority, optional reason and source. AI may reorder, raise priorities and explain required actions, but cannot delete/alter their source or fabricate extra model-specific actions. D14 explains this bounded synthesis and generation freshness. ChecklistChecks adds 18 JUnit groups and ChecklistGuiSmoke verifies the real Swing controls. Total: 168 passing JUnit tests; live F10 unverified. SD05's F11 answer/update/resume loop remains outside scope.
